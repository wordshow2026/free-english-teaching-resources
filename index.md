
# Free English Teaching Resources for ESL Teachers

Welcome to a growing collection of free English teaching resources designed for ESL and EFL teachers, tutors, and educators.

Whether you are preparing a vocabulary lesson, teaching English grammar, or looking for engaging classroom activities, these resources can help you plan lessons and make learning more enjoyable.

## More Interactive English Learning Activities

Looking for ways to turn vocabulary lists, grammar topics, and lesson materials into interactive learning activities?

Visit [WordShow](https://wordshow.net), an online platform where teachers can create interactive educational games and classroom activities.

## Who Are These Resources For?

These resources are suitable for:

- ESL and EFL teachers
- English tutors and private instructors
- Primary and secondary school teachers
- Language learners who want to practise English independently

## About This Project

This repository is maintained as an open collection of practical English teaching materials. New topics, lesson ideas, and classroom activities may be added over time.

Teachers and educators are welcome to use these materials, share them, and suggest improvements.
