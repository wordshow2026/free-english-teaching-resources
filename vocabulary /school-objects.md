# School Objects: English Vocabulary

School objects are things that students and teachers use in the classroom. Learning their names helps students talk about their school supplies, classroom environment, and daily activities.

This resource introduces 30 common school objects with IPA pronunciation, simple definitions, example sentences, and classroom activities. It is suitable for beginner English learners (A1–A2).

## 3. Useful Sentences About School Objects

Students can use these expressions to talk about objects in their school bags and classrooms.

- What is this? — It is a pencil.
- What are these? — They are scissors.
- Is this your notebook? — Yes, it is.
- Do you have a ruler? — No, I don't.
- Where is my pencil case? — It is on the desk.
- How many pencils do you have? — I have three pencils.

### Grammar Tip: This, That, These, and Those

Use **this** and **that** for one object:

- This is my pen. (near)
- That is your bag. (far)

Use **these** and **those** for more than one object:

- These are my books. (near)
- Those are classroom chairs. (far)

## 4. Classroom Activities

### Activity 1: What's in My School Bag?

**Objective:** Practice naming school objects and using simple sentences.

**Instructions:**

1. Students put several school objects inside their bags.
2. One student takes out an object without showing it to the class.
3. The student gives clues about the object.
4. Classmates guess what it is.

Example:

- Student A: You use it to write.
- Student B: Is it a pen?
- Student A: Yes, that's right!

### Activity 2: Classroom Object Hunt

**Objective:** Improve vocabulary recognition and listening skills.

**Instructions:**

1. Divide students into small groups.
2. Give each group a list of school objects.
3. Students look around the classroom and find the objects.
4. The group that correctly identifies the most objects wins.

Example list:

- Something you write with.
- Something you use to erase pencil marks.
- Something you sit on.
- Something a teacher writes on.
- Something you use to measure a line.

### Activity 3: Describe an Object

**Objective:** Practice speaking using adjectives and simple descriptions.

Ask students to choose one school object and describe it without saying its name.

Example:

- It is small.
- It is usually made of wood or plastic.
- You use it to write.
- You can erase what you write with it.

Answer: Pencil.

## 5. Vocabulary Practice

Choose the correct answer.

**1. Which object is used to remove pencil marks?**

- A. Ruler
- B. Eraser
- C. Marker
- D. Folder

**2. Which object is used to measure length?**

- A. Ruler
- B. Notebook
- C. Glue
- D. Crayon

**3. What do students use to carry their books?**

- A. Calculator
- B. Blackboard
- C. Backpack
- D. Projector

**4. Which object is used to cut paper?**

- A. Glue
- B. Scissors
- C. Highlighter
- D. Pencil case

**5. What does a teacher use to write on a whiteboard?**

- A. Chalk
- B. Eraser
- C. Board marker
- D. Sharpener

### Answer Key

1. B — Eraser
2. A — Ruler
3. C — Backpack
4. B — Scissors
5. C — Board marker

## 6. Teaching Tips

- Bring real school objects into the classroom so students can see and touch them.
- Use flashcards to introduce new words and practice pronunciation.
- Encourage students to name objects they can see around the classroom.
- Practice singular and plural forms, especially irregular or commonly confusing words such as scissors.
- Use short matching games and quizzes to review vocabulary regularly.
