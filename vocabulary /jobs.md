# Jobs and Occupations: English Vocabulary

Learning vocabulary about jobs and occupations helps students talk about people's work, describe different professions, and discuss future career plans.

This resource introduces 30 common job titles with simple definitions, example sentences, and classroom activities. It is suitable for beginner to pre-intermediate English learners (A1–A2).

## 3. Useful Sentences About Jobs

Students can use these sentence patterns to talk about occupations.

- What do you do? — I'm a teacher.
- What does your father do? — He is a doctor.
- Where does a chef work? — A chef works in a restaurant.
- What does a firefighter do? — A firefighter puts out fires.
- What would you like to be? — I'd like to be a pilot.

### Grammar Tip: A and An with Jobs

Use **a** before a job that begins with a consonant sound:

- a teacher
- a doctor
- a firefighter
- a programmer

Use **an** before a job that begins with a vowel sound:

- an engineer
- an artist
- an accountant
- an actor

## 4. Classroom Activities

### Activity 1: Guess My Job

**Objective:** Practice job vocabulary and speaking skills.

**Instructions:**

1. One student chooses a job without telling the class.
2. Other students ask yes/no questions.
3. The student answers until someone guesses the job.

Example:

- Student A: Do you work in a hospital?
- Student B: No, I don't.
- Student A: Do you work with food?
- Student B: Yes, I do.
- Student A: Are you a chef?
- Student B: Yes, I am!

### Activity 2: Match the Job to Its Description

**Objective:** Improve vocabulary recognition.

Match each job with the correct description.

| Job | Description |
|---|---|
| 1. Teacher | A. Repairs cars. |
| 2. Mechanic | B. Takes care of sick people. |
| 3. Nurse | C. Teaches students. |
| 4. Pilot | D. Flies an aircraft. |
| 5. Baker | E. Makes bread and cakes. |

**Answers:** 1–C, 2–A, 3–B, 4–D, 5–E.

### Activity 3: My Dream Job

**Objective:** Practice writing and speaking about future careers.

Ask students to complete these sentences:

1. My dream job is to be a __________.
2. I want to be a __________ because __________.
3. A __________ usually works in __________.
4. A __________ needs to be __________.

Students can then share their answers with a partner or present them to the class.

## 6. Teaching Tips

- Use pictures of different professionals to introduce new vocabulary.
- Ask students to mime a job while classmates guess the occupation.
- Encourage students to describe jobs using simple present tense.
- Connect job vocabulary to students' interests and future ambitions.
- Revisit the vocabulary through short quizzes and matching games.
