
# Present Simple Tense: English Grammar Guide

The present simple tense is one of the most commonly used tenses in English. It is used to describe daily routines, habits, general truths, permanent situations, and regular activities.

This resource explains how to form and use the present simple tense, with clear examples, common mistakes, classroom activities, and practice exercises. It is suitable for beginner to elementary English learners (A1–A2).

## 1. When Do We Use the Present Simple?

We use the present simple in several situations.

### A. Daily Routines and Habits

Use the present simple to describe things that happen regularly.

Examples:

- I wake up at 6 a.m. every day.
- She goes to school by bus.
- They play football every weekend.
- My father reads the newspaper every morning.

### B. General Truths and Facts

Use the present simple for facts that are generally or scientifically true.

Examples:

- The sun rises in the east.
- Water boils at 100°C at sea level.
- Birds have wings.
- The Earth moves around the sun.

### C. Permanent Situations

Use the present simple to describe relatively permanent situations.

Examples:

- She lives in Hanoi.
- My brother works at a hospital.
- They study English at school.
- He speaks three languages.

### D. Timetables and Schedules

Use the present simple for fixed schedules and timetables.

Examples:

- The train leaves at 8 a.m.
- The English class starts at 9 o'clock.
- The shop opens at 7:30 every morning.

## 2. How to Form the Present Simple

The form of the present simple depends on whether the sentence uses an ordinary verb or the verb "to be".

### A. Affirmative Sentences

**Structure:**

- I / You / We / They + base verb
- He / She / It + verb-s or verb-es

Examples:

| Subject | Verb | Example |
|---|---|---|
| I | play | I play tennis. |
| You | work | You work hard. |
| We | study | We study English. |
| They | live | They live in Vietnam. |
| He | plays | He plays tennis. |
| She | works | She works in a bank. |
| It | runs | It runs quickly. |

### B. Spelling Rules for He, She, and It

Most verbs take **-s**:

- play → plays
- read → reads
- work → works

Add **-es** to verbs ending in -s, -sh, -ch, -x, or -o:

- watch → watches
- wash → washes
- go → goes
- fix → fixes
- pass → passes

For verbs ending in a consonant + y, change **y** to **-ies**:

- study → studies
- try → tries
- carry → carries

For verbs ending in a vowel + y, simply add **-s**:

- play → plays
- enjoy → enjoys
- buy → buys

Some verbs have irregular forms:

- have → has
- do → does

### C. Negative Sentences

Use **do not (don't)** or **does not (doesn't)** before the base verb.

**Structure:**

- I / You / We / They + don't + base verb
- He / She / It + doesn't + base verb

Examples:

- I don't drink coffee.
- They don't work on Sundays.
- She doesn't like spicy food.
- He doesn't play basketball.

**Important:** After "doesn't", always use the base form of the verb.

Correct: She doesn't like pizza.

Incorrect: She doesn't likes pizza.

### D. Yes/No Questions

Use **Do** or **Does** at the beginning of the question.

**Structure:**

- Do + I / you / we / they + base verb?
- Does + he / she / it + base verb?

Examples:

- Do you speak English?
- Do they live near here?
- Does she work in a hospital?
- Does he play football?

Short answers:

- Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
- Yes, they do. / No, they don't.
- Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.

### E. Wh-Questions

Use question words such as What, Where, When, Why, and How with Do or Does.

Examples:

- What do you do after school?
- Where does your mother work?
- When do they have lunch?
- Why does he study English?
- How do you go to work?

## 3. The Present Simple with "To Be"

The verb "to be" has three forms in the present simple: **am, is, and are**.

| Subject | To Be | Example |
|---|---|---|
| I | am | I am a student. |
| He / She / It | is | She is happy. |
| You / We / They | are | They are teachers. |

### Negative Forms

- I am not tired.
- He isn't at home.
- We aren't busy.

### Questions

- Are you a student?
- Is she your sister?
- Are they ready?

**Important:** Do not use do or does with the verb "to be".

Correct: Is he a teacher?

Incorrect: Does he be a teacher?

## 4. Common Time Expressions

These expressions often appear with the present simple.

| Expression | Example |
|---|---|
| Every day | I exercise every day. |
| Every morning | She drinks milk every morning. |
| Every week | We have an English lesson every week. |
| On Mondays | He plays football on Mondays. |
| At weekends | They visit their grandparents at weekends. |
| Once a week | I go swimming once a week. |
| Twice a month | She visits the dentist twice a month. |
| Usually | We usually have dinner at home. |
| Often | He often reads books. |
| Never | I never smoke. |

## 5. Common Grammar Mistakes

| Incorrect | Correct | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| She work in a school. | She works in a school. | Add -s for he, she, and it in affirmative sentences. |
| He don't like tea. | He doesn't like tea. | Use doesn't with he, she, and it. |
| She doesn't likes coffee. | She doesn't like coffee. | Use the base verb after doesn't. |
| Do he play tennis? | Does he play tennis? | Use does for he, she, and it. |
| I am go to school every day. | I go to school every day. | Do not add am before an ordinary present simple verb. |
| Does she is happy? | Is she happy? | Use the verb to be directly in questions. |

## 6. Classroom Activities

### Activity 1: My Daily Routine

**Objective:** Practice describing daily habits using the present simple.

**Instructions:**

1. Ask students to write down five things they do every day.
2. Students use the present simple to describe their routines.
3. Put students into pairs and ask them to share their sentences.
4. Invite a few students to present their routines to the class.

Example:

- I wake up at 6:30.
- I have breakfast at 7:00.
- I go to school at 7:30.
- I do my homework in the evening.
- I go to bed at 10:00.

### Activity 2: Find Someone Who...

**Objective:** Practice forming present simple questions.

**Instructions:**

1. Give students a list of activities.
2. Students walk around the classroom and ask classmates questions.
3. They write down the names of classmates who answer "Yes".
4. Ask students to report their findings using complete sentences.

Suggested questions:

- Do you play football?
- Do you read books every day?
- Do you drink milk?
- Do you watch television in the evening?
- Do you speak another language?

Example report:

- Minh plays football.
- Anna reads books every day.
- David doesn't drink milk.

### Activity 3: Correct the Mistake

**Objective:** Identify and correct common present simple errors.

**Instructions:**

1. Write incorrect sentences on the board.
2. Ask students to work in small groups.
3. Each group rewrites the sentences correctly.
4. Review the answers together.

Sentences:

1. She go to school every day.
2. He don't like vegetables.
3. Does your father works here?
4. They doesn't play tennis.
5. My mother cook dinner every evening.

**Answers:**

1. She goes to school every day.
2. He doesn't like vegetables.
3. Does your father work here?
4. They don't play tennis.
5. My mother cooks dinner every evening.

## 8. Teaching Tips

- Use daily routine pictures to introduce common present simple verbs.
- Teach the third-person singular spelling rules with plenty of examples.
- Compare affirmative, negative, and question forms side by side.
- Encourage students to talk about their real routines and habits.
- Use information-gap activities so students have a reason to ask questions.
- Review the difference between ordinary verbs and the verb "to be".
