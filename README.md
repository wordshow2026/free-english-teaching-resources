# Free English Teaching Resources

A growing collection of free English teaching resources for ESL and EFL teachers. Explore vocabulary lists, grammar explanations, classroom games, and practical activities designed to make English lessons more engaging.

Whether you teach young learners, teenagers, or adults, these resources can help you prepare lessons and create meaningful learning experiences.

## 🎯 How to Use These Resources

Teachers can use these materials to:

- Introduce new vocabulary topics.
- Prepare warm-up and revision activities.
- Create classroom quizzes and group challenges.
- Support individual and collaborative learning.
- Give students additional practice outside the classroom.

Feel free to adapt the materials to suit your students' age, English level, and learning objectives.

## 🎮 Create Interactive English Activities

Looking for ways to turn vocabulary lists, lesson notes, or teaching materials into interactive classroom games?

[WordShow](https://wordshow.net) is an online learning platform that helps teachers create quizzes, vocabulary games, and interactive learning activities. Teachers can use AI to generate activities from a topic, text, or image, then share them with students.
